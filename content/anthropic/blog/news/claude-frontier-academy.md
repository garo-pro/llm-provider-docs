Title: Anthropic invests $100 million to train 10,000 engineers and tackle the enterprise AI talent gap

URL Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-frontier-academy

Markdown Content:
Today we are launching Claude Frontier Academy to solve one of the most pressing issues in AI implementation: talent. Backed by a $100 million commitment, Anthropic aims to train 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers (FDEs) by the end of 2027. Engineers from Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley and Novo Nordisk are in the first cohorts.

Every enterprise is racing to bring AI into its organization, but the people with the skills to make it work inside a real business have become the hardest talent to find. Inside the companies we work with, a small group of deeply skilled people drives an outsized share of what AI delivers. The Academy is the first of its kind from an AI company, built from our own work deploying Claude inside the world’s largest enterprises and the professional services organizations that serve them.

“A small team of high-agency people with the right skills, access to Claude, and a deep understanding of how their business runs can transform an entire company. The hard part is building that depth of talent, engineers with the highest level of AI fluency who can take Claude from an idea to a system in production and turn it into faster processes and new products and services. No AI company has invested in developing that talent inside its customers and partners at this depth. Claude Frontier Academy trains people the way our own engineers learn, and we want those who graduate to set the standard for how AI gets built inside a business.” – Steve Corfield, Global Head of Business Development and Partnerships at Anthropic

### The first program trains 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers

The Academy’s first program, the Frontier Deployed Engineer Residency, follows the medical model for building expertise. Doctors learn from practicing physicians, train on real cases, and are assessed before they practice alone. Likewise, FDE Residency engineers learn from practitioners, practice on realistic cases, and are assessed on their work before they earn the credential. The goal is agentic systems that change how a business runs, from faster, redesigned processes to new products and services.

The program is for hands-on software engineers with strong fundamentals, a record of building with LLMs, experience helping others adopt AI, and a habit of tackling their business’s most important problems. Organizations nominate their strongest engineers, each arriving with a named Claude project to lead when they return. Prior experience building AI agents isn’t required.

Engineers start with a multi-day in-person program with Anthropic engineers and licensed instructors. They work through a simulated enterprise deployment, from the right use case through security review to handover, and finish with a graded practical on a new scenario. Those who pass earn the Claude Resident Engineer badge and move into the 12-week residency, where they lead a real Claude use case at their own organization, supported by Anthropic engineers and learning from their cohort as they build. Engineers are assessed again at the end of the 12 weeks, and those who pass earn the Claude Frontier Deployed Engineer badge, with the first expected in early 2027.

### The first cohorts are running now

Cohorts are running in San Francisco, New York and London. The program will grow toward 10,000 engineers by the end of 2027. Participation is by nomination; organizations can ask their Anthropic account team or Partner Account Manager whether they’re eligible.

Claude Frontier Academy builds on the [Claude Partner Network](https://claude.com/blog/four-role-based-claude-certifications), where professionals across 46,000 firms have earned more than 175,000 Claude certifications and nearly 4,000 people have completed Basecamp, an immersive program that teaches the same curriculum our Applied AI teams receive when they onboard. Claude Frontier Academy is for the people who will lead the work, and training 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers is the first step of many.
