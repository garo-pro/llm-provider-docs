# Product feeds

> For the complete documentation index, see [llms.txt](/llms.txt). Markdown versions of documentation pages are available by appending `.md` to the page URL.

> Surface accurate, up-to-date catalog data for discovery and checkout in ChatGPT.

Product feeds provide structured catalog data that helps ChatGPT surface the right products with accurate pricing, availability, and seller context.
